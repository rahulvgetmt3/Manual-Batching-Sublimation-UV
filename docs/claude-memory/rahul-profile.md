Rahul Verma (rahulv@getmt3.com) works at **MT3 / Casely** on print production operations. That covers HP SiteFlow, the Pulse DB, the UV and print teams, and Amazon FBM. He isn't a developer: his manager set up the environment, and he works on a Mac (`~/Dev`) in VS Code.

**How he works:**
- He checks results visually, in Photoshop and Illustrator screenshots, and reports issues that way. Show him concrete evidence, such as a test run name and what he should see in which panel.
- He gives short, rapid change requests and often adds to them mid-task.
- He likes a plain explanation of the cause before the fix.
- He expects the outputs to match the team's existing manual files exactly.
